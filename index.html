Economist · Applied Researcher · Bogotá, Colombia

I am a Colombian economist completing a Master's in Economics at Universidad del Rosario, where I also work as a Junior Researcher. My work focuses on health economics, labor markets, and the causal effects of public policy in Latin America, with a particular interest in how institutions and market structure shape inequality and access for vulnerable populations.

I am currently a data analyst at ADRES — Colombia's national health insurance fund — where I build analytical pipelines and monitoring systems covering over 24 million beneficiaries. I am a member of Colfuturo's Semillero de Talentos (2025) and am preparing to apply to PhD programs in economics.
